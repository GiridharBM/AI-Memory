## Long Answer Questions

**1. Explain the process of how artificial intelligence works.**

Artificial intelligence works through a series of steps starting with data collection, where raw data is gathered from various sources. This data is then processed to clean, format, and prepare it for analysis. Next, algorithms are used to identify patterns in the data, allowing the AI system to learn and improve its performance over time. Finally, the model uses these learned patterns to make decisions or predictions, which constitute the final output. The entire process is often visualized using a flowchart, which is a common topic in exams.

**2. Describe the importance of the learning step in AI systems.**

The learning step is crucial in AI systems because it enables the system to identify patterns in data, which are used to improve its performance. Through this process, AI systems can adapt and refine their algorithms based on new data, leading to more accurate predictions and decisions over time. This continuous learning is what allows AI to become more effective with experience.
