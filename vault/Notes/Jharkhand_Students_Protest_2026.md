# Jharkhand_Students_Protest_2026

## Summary

Jharkhand students protest over recruitment exam irregularities, entering 21st day with Tiranga Yatra planned for August 15.

Students in Jharkhand are protesting against alleged irregularities in recruitment examinations, entering their 21st day of demonstrations. The protest, led by JPSC-JSSC Reforms Manch, calls for transparency, cancellation of tests, and an independent CBI probe. Demonstrators plan a Tiranga Yatra on August 15 to mark India's 80th Independence Day. The Ranchi district administration has lodged an FIR against 300 unnamed protesters for violence during the August 10 march. Police are investigating video footage to identify those involved in the violence. The protest has received support from social organisations and authors, with free food being provided. The Chief Minister has committed to cancelling the 14th JPSC examination. The BJP and Congress have accused each other of politicising the movement. Devendra Nath Mahto, a hunger striker, is under medical treatment after his condition worsened during the protest.
