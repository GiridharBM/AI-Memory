# Our Organization

## Summary

Utthunga, founded in 2007, provides engineering R&D and industrial solutions to global clients across multiple sectors. The company has grown from a small team to 1,200+ engineers with offices in 8 locations.

## Detailed Summary

Utthunga, established in 2007, delivers end-to-end engineering R&D services and industrial solutions to leading OEMs, ISVs, and industries such as Energy, Chemicals, Metals, Mining, Pharmaceuticals, Power, and Utilities. With 18+ years of experience, the company has grown from a small one-room office to a global team of 1,200+ engineers across 8 locations. Utthunga offers turnkey solutions in automation, sustainability, smart manufacturing, digital twin, safety, and electrification. The company adheres to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 27001:2013 standards, ensuring quality and information security. Core values include Innovation with Purpose, Integrity and Ownership, Continuous Improvement, Respect and Care, and a One Team mindset. The CEO, Krishnan K M, emphasizes sustainability and driving impact through engineering innovation. Utthunga's services span consulting, engineering, production support, and content engineering, aligning with the customer's value chain.

## Executive Summary

Utthunga is a global engineering and industrial solutions provider founded in 2007, offering services across multiple industries. The company emphasizes innovation, sustainability, and customer-centric approaches, operating under ISO standards and employing multidisciplinary engineering to deliver turnkey solutions.
