# Artificial Intelligence Basics

## Summary

Artificial Intelligence (AI) involves creating machines that mimic human intelligence. Machine Learning and Deep Learning are subsets of AI, with applications in chatbots, recommendation systems, and autonomous vehicles.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of building machines capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. Machine Learning is a subset of AI where algorithms learn patterns from data. Deep Learning, a further subset, uses neural networks with many layers. AI has various applications such as chatbots, recommendation systems, autonomous vehicles, and medical diagnosis. Key terms include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Neural Networks, Deep Learning, and Large Language Models. References include Andrew Ng's Machine Learning course and Stanford CS229.
