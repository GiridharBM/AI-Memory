# Our-Organization

## Summary

Utthunga, founded in 2007, offers engineering R&D services to global industries. They have 1,200+ engineers and 8 global offices.

Utthunga, established in 2007, provides end-to-end engineering R&D services and industrial solutions to leading OEMs, ISVs, and industries like Energy, Chemicals, Metals, Mining, Pharmaceuticals, Power, and Utilities. With 18+ years of expertise, they offer turnkey solutions in automation, sustainability, digital twin, and more. The company has 1,200+ engineers across 8 global offices, serving 150+ clients. Their Core Values include Innovation with Purpose, Integrity and Ownership, Relentless Learning, Respect and Care, and a One Team mindset. They are ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 27001:2013 certified, with a US headquarters and presence in India, Germany, and Japan.
