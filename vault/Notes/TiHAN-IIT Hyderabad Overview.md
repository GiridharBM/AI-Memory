# TiHAN-IIT Hyderabad Overview

## Summary

TiHAN-IIT Hyderabad is India's first autonomous navigation testbed, supporting R&D in unmanned systems and smart mobility.

TiHAN-IIT Hyderabad, established as India's first autonomous navigation testbed, focuses on terrestrial and aerial autonomous navigation systems. It operates under the DST-supported National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems (NM-ICPS) as a Technology Innovation Hub (TIH). The initiative supports IIT Hyderabad's M.Tech in Smart Mobility program to develop next-gen talent. TiHAN also contributes to the university's deep-tech startup ecosystem, with over 40+ startups linked to its initiatives. It has deployed an autonomous campus shuttle as a real-world application and collaborates with industry and research partners to translate innovations into practical deployments.
