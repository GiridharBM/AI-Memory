## Who We Are

### Transformation Partner for Engineering Innovation and Enterprise Excellence

**Founded in 2007**, Utthunga delivers end-to-end engineering R&D services and industrial solutions to leading OEMs, ISVs, MSPs, and industries including Energy, Chemicals, Metals, Mining, Pharmaceuticals, Power, and Utilities.

With **18+ years of industrial expertise**, our Industrial Center of Excellence and innovation labs empower global companies to launch pioneering, future-ready solutions faster – without compromising quality.

Our **1,200+ multidisciplinary engineers** provide capabilities from design and development to sensor-to-cloud integration, spanning product, digital, process, and plant engineering. We offer turnkey solutions in automation, sustainability, smart manufacturing, digital twin, safety, and electrification, along with complete manufacturing services through our global ecosystem – helping OEMs and industrial enterprises scale efficiently while ensuring quality and compliance.

## Growth Story

Our journey began in 2007 in a small one-room office, driven by a clear and ambitious vision – to meet the evolving technology needs of the industrial world. With a strong foundation in industrial connectivity and protocols, we started by connecting devices and systems, laying the groundwork for what would become a remarkable journey.

Today, Utthunga is a 1200+ strong team of engineers across 8 global offices, serving 150+ clients across diverse industries. Our unique strength lies in the powerful blend of IT, OT, and ET capabilities backed by deep domain knowledge, allowing us to solve complex engineering challenges with speed and precision.

Customer obsession is at our core. By understanding client needs, we deliver impactful solutions, backed by value engineering and continuous re-skilling – empowering our people to build reliable, future-ready products and accelerate time-to-market.
